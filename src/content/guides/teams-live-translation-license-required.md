This is the single most misunderstood requirement in the category, and it is usually discovered after a budget has been approved.

## The requirement

**Microsoft 365 Copilot.** Not Teams Premium.

Teams Premium — around $10 per user per month on annual billing — is **not sufficient** for Interpreter. The prerequisite is Microsoft 365 Copilot, roughly $30 per user per month.

For organisations that budgeted for Premium, the real entry cost is about three times what was planned. Microsoft documents this in the [Interpreter agent admin prerequisites](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/interpreter-agent-teams).

## The three other limits, all often discovered late

**1. External guests and anonymous users are excluded.** Anyone joining without a licensed account in your tenant cannot use it. That covers most client calls, supplier calls, candidate interviews and partner meetings.

**2. It does not work in ad hoc meetings, webinars or town halls.** Scheduled meetings only. If your case is the unscheduled call or the all-hands, Interpreter is not available regardless of licensing.

**3. Recordings do not keep it.** Microsoft's own [support documentation](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/teams/copilot/interpreter-in-microsoft-teams-meetings-and-calls) states that meeting recordings capture only the original meeting audio and do not include interpretation audio, and that translated transcripts disappear when the meeting ends.

For a distributed team where most people watch afterwards, that last one removes most of the value — and it is the limit least often checked before purchase.

## Language coverage

**10 languages.** Check the current list against your actual needs rather than assuming coverage; teams spanning Polish, Turkish or Vietnamese frequently find themselves outside it.

## When Teams Interpreter is the right choice

- Internal meetings only
- Two languages, both supported
- Everyone already has Microsoft 365 Copilot for other reasons
- Scheduled, not ad hoc
- The live session matters; the recording does not

If you have already bought Copilot, the marginal cost is zero and you should use it. That is a real scenario in large enterprises.

## If any of those do not hold

Buying Copilot solely to get translation is an expensive route to a feature with four significant limits. Before doing it, price the alternative honestly against your actual meetings — particularly if they involve external participants, more than two languages, or recordings anyone watches later.
