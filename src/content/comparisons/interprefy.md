## What Interprefy does well

Interprefy is a hybrid vendor: AI translation and human interpreters in the same platform, with the ability to run both. That combination is genuinely useful, and it is something we do not offer at all.

Coverage is broad — **82 languages fully supported, with roughly 19 more partially**. It is a Swiss company, which is a real advantage in European procurement where the counterparty's jurisdiction is part of the assessment.

For a regulated organisation running large scheduled events that occasionally need a human in the loop, Interprefy is a well-matched product.

## The structural point about its pricing

Interprefy **does not publish prices**, and its [pricing page](https://www.interprefy.com/pricing) describes a model where **cost scales with the number of languages** as well as with hours.

That is a rational way to price a conference. It is a poor fit for a specific shape of meeting: a small group where four languages are live simultaneously. In a conference, four languages means four interpreter channels serving hundreds of listeners, and per-language pricing tracks the cost of delivery. In a four-person call where everyone speaks a different language, per-language pricing charges you four times for a conversation that only has four people in it.

If your meetings are that shape and they happen often, this is the line item to model before signing.

**Unused hours do not roll over.** Combined with a purchase made through a sales conversation rather than against observed usage, irregular usage pays for capacity it does not consume.

## When each one wins

**Interprefy** — large scheduled events, human interpreters needed or wanted, European counterparty required, predictable hours, few languages per event.

**VoxTranslate** — small groups, several languages at once, short notice, irregular usage, and a preference for knowing the price before the conversation.

The per-language pricing question is the one worth asking them directly. If your typical meeting has four languages, get that quoted before anything else.
