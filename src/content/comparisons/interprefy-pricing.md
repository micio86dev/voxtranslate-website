## What Interprefy charges

**Interprefy does not publish prices.** There is no price list, no per-minute rate and no plan comparison. The [pricing page](https://www.interprefy.com/pricing) routes to a sales conversation.

What is documented about the model is more useful than a number would be:

- Pricing **scales with the number of languages** as well as with hours
- **Unused hours do not roll over**

Both facts are load-bearing when you model your own cost.

## Why the language-based component matters

Charging by language is a reasonable way to price a conference. Each language is an interpreter channel and a delivery cost, serving many listeners.

It is a poor fit for a small multilingual conversation. A four-person call in four languages carries four language charges for a meeting with four people in it — where at a conference those same four languages might serve four hundred.

If your meetings are that shape and recur, model this before signing. It is the single line item most likely to surprise you, and it is the one Interprefy's structure handles worst.

## Why "no rollover" matters

Hours are bought in advance, against a forecast made before you have observed your own usage. If usage is regular, this is fine. If it is lumpy — a heavy quarter and a quiet one — the quiet quarter's hours are gone.

The combination of a sales-set commitment and no rollover means the risk of forecasting wrong sits entirely with the buyer.

## What to ask them

If you are getting a quote, these are the questions that determine your real cost:

1. **What is the per-language charge, and how is a "language" counted** — per event, per session, or per active pair?
2. **What is the minimum commitment**, in hours and in months?
3. **What happens to unused hours** at the end of the term, in writing?
4. **Is AI-only priced differently** from sessions with human interpreters?
5. **Is there a per-session minimum** — does a 20-minute call bill as an hour?

Question 5 catches out anyone planning to use an event platform for short meetings.
