## What Boostlingo does well

Boostlingo is human-first. Its core is professional interpretation, with access to **more than 275 languages** through interpreters, alongside an AI product — [Boostlingo AI Pro](https://boostlingo.com/boostlingo-ai-pro/) — covering **130+ languages**.

The human language reach is the number that matters. No AI translation product covers 275 languages at usable quality, and for rare and low-resource languages the gap is large and closing slowly.

For healthcare, legal and public-sector settings where the standard is defined by profession rather than by convenience, this is the right category of vendor.

## Where the fit breaks

Boostlingo is built around interpretation as a service. That means booking, scheduling and a per-session model, which suits sessions that are planned and consequential.

It does not suit the meetings that make up most of a distributed company's week — short, frequent, unscheduled, and currently happening with no translation at all. Nobody books an interpreter for a fifteen-minute standup, and a product built around booking does not become the answer for meetings that will never be booked.

## Pricing

**Boostlingo does not publish prices.** As with every human-first vendor in this market, cost depends on language pair, duration, notice period and whether interpreters are on-demand or scheduled — which is why it is quoted rather than listed.

That is defensible for interpretation. It does mean you cannot evaluate the option without a conversation.
